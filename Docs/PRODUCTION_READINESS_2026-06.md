# LIT-ISO — Production Readiness Plan (2026-06-24)

> Generated by full codebase review: 4 parallel agent audits covering game feel/juice,
> core systems, world/art wiring, and UI/UX. All findings are grounded in specific
> file paths, method names, and observed code state — not assumptions.
>
> **TL;DR:** The systems are further along than they appear. Three clusters of work
> separate this from a production-quality build: (A) close the Trial loop (2 wires
> away from working end-to-end), (B) add combat juice (all combat is currently
> silent and visually dead), and (C) fix a handful of specific art gaps. Everything
> else is polish layered on a solid foundation.

---

## What is actually in good shape

Before the gap list, it is worth being explicit about what the audits confirmed is
**real and production-quality**, because the codebase is further along than the
docs suggest:

**Worldgen** is genuine. The terrain sampler uses multi-octave Perlin, Whittaker
climate (temperature × moisture biome selection), jittered-Voronoi province
coherence, warped river bands, biome blend bands with 8-direction crossfades, and
settlement stamping — none of this is prototype. 136 tile PNGs and 233 decoration
PNGs are on disk. Props are placed in clustered groups (groves, outcrops, bush
bands), not uniform scatter.

**Save / Load** is fully implemented (v11, `FoundationBootstrap.Save`/`Load`,
atomic `.tmp`/`.bak` writes, `JsonUtility` round-trip). It covers player pos,
inventory, equipment, hotbar, full progression + trial lifecycle + affinities +
skills + quests, world modifications, placeables, storage, crops, dungeon state,
explored map, day/night time, mob positions. This is not stubbed.

**Trial scoring backend** is complete. `GradeForTotal()`, `CompleteTrial()`,
`BuildClassOffers()`, `BuildProfessionOffers()`, save/restore of
`FoundationTrialLifecycleSaveData` — all exist and work. The trial loop is two
wires away from being fully playable end-to-end (see §1 below).

**Survival systems** are real. `FoundationCampingSystem` has ward suppression with
tier/breach probability, recovery multipliers, night fatigue penalties, and
`RestAt()` wired to `PlayerInteraction` (right-click on campsite). `MobSpawner`
calls `mob.ApplyNightDanger()` with damage/speed/aggro multipliers at spawn.
`FoundationDeathSystem` fades to black, finds the nearest campfire, and respawns
at 50% HP — functional without a death penalty. STR→Jump is also fully implemented.

**Visual polish systems** are wired: `SpriteAmbient.Material` applied to every
ground tile, `DecorationShadow` driven by `DayNightSystem.LightFromDir`,
`WindSway` on vegetation, `FoundationContactShadow` on all props,
`PropOcclusionFader` hiding props that overlap the player, `FoundationDepthPolish`
wiring all three on spawn.

**UI layer** is substantial: 9-tab character panel, toast notification stack with
colour-coded accent bars by message type, quest tracker, `TrialStatusBanner`
showing day/forecast live, transmigration cutscene on first entry,
`ClassAssignmentView` (the void ceremony) is visually complete (Ken Burns
background, typewriter header, rank letter, skill-point count, 4 axis receipts,
3 class offer cards with rarity borders).

## The gaps — ranked by impact on production feel

### TIER 1 — Highest impact, low-to-medium effort

#### 1. Close the Trial loop: wire day-7 to the void ceremony (Est: 2 days)

This is the game's signature moment and it is two wires away from working.

**What exists:** `FoundationProgression.CompleteTrial()` builds class/profession
offers and snapshots the grade. `ClassAssignmentView` renders the ceremony
beautifully. `PlaceholderClassAssignmentViewModel` provides test data (F8 debug).

**What's missing:**
- `DayNightSystem` has no `OnDayCompleted` event. Nothing calls
  `Progression.AdvanceTrialDay()` when a calendar day passes. The only callers
  are the editor validator. Players cannot reach day 7 organically.
- `FoundationClassAssignmentAdapter` does not exist. `ClassAssignmentView.Show()`
  is called with hardcoded placeholder data from `GamePanelsController`.

**Fix:**
1. In `DayNightSystem`, track when `_time` wraps past midnight and fire
   `public event Action OnDayCompleted`. Wire it in `FoundationProgressionHooks`
   or `FoundationBootstrap` to call `Progression.AdvanceTrialDay()`.
2. Write `FoundationClassAssignmentAdapter : IClassAssignmentViewModel` (~60
   lines) that reads `Progression.CaptureTrialReadState().classOffers` and calls
   `Progression.SelectClassOffer(id)` on confirm. Subscribe
   `Progression.TrialCompleted` → `ClassAssignmentView.Show(new
   FoundationClassAssignmentAdapter(Progression))`.

Without this, the 7-day frame — the game's identity — is unreachable in a real
playthrough.

---

#### 2. Add combat audio and hit feedback (Est: 1 day code + audio drops)

All combat is currently silent and visually dead.

**What's missing:**
- `SfxManager.Play("hit")` is called on every combat hit (Mob.TakeMobDamage,
  Mob.TryAttack, FoundationDeathSystem) but `Assets/Resources/Audio/SFX/hit.wav`
  does not exist — all combat is silent.
- No `PlayerAnimator` hurt/attack states. The player sprite never changes
  during combat. `PlayerInteraction` calls `_heldTool?.Swing()` but there is no
  PlayerAnimator hook for it.
- `Mob._hurt` and `Mob._die` arrays exist and are loaded, but `TakeMobDamage`
  destroys the mob immediately on death — the die animation never plays.
- No hit-flash: `_sr.color` is never written during combat in either `Mob.cs` or
  the player path.
- No screen shake anywhere in the game.
- `WorldFx` has `Debris`, `Dust`, `Smoke`, `Trail` — but nothing fires on mob hit.
  No `Spark`/`Impact` burst on melee contact.

**Fixes by priority:**
1. Drop `hit.wav` into `Resources/Audio/SFX/` → instant audio for all combat.
2. Add `WorldFx.Spark()` (reuse Debris with a warm tint + upward velocity) and
   call it from `Mob.TakeMobDamage()` and `FoundationPlayerStats.TakeDamage()`.
3. Add a `HitFlash()` coroutine to `Mob` and player that tweens `_sr.color` →
   white over 0.07 s then back. Call from `TakeMobDamage`.
4. Add a camera shake impulse in `FoundationBootstrap` (`ShakeCamera(strength,
   duration)` using a manual offset coroutine) and call it on player damage.
5. Defer mob death for `0.35 s` to let the die frames play before destroying.

#### 3. Fix the GameSettingsMenu key conflict (Est: 30 minutes)

`GameSettingsMenu` and `GamePanelsController` both bind `KeyCode.I`. At runtime
one silently wins. The settings panel (which includes screen shake toggle, camera
presets, and debug overlay) becomes unreachable.

**Fix:** Change `GameSettingsMenu.toggleKey` to `KeyCode.O` (or collapse it into
the `CharacterPanelTab.Settings` tab that already exists in `CharacterPanelView`
but is not yet implemented — the cleaner long-term solution).

---

#### 4. Wire the Trial UI adapter — make F8 trigger fire from real data (Est: 0.5 days)

Even before the day-7 wire (§1), the ceremony should show real data. Currently
`ClassAssignmentView` always shows `PlaceholderClassAssignmentViewModel` (B rank,
4 points, 3 hardcoded classes), regardless of what the player has actually done.

**Fix:** Create `FoundationClassAssignmentAdapter` as described in §1 and call
`GamePanelsController`'s F8 path with it. This makes the ceremony immediately
useful for playtesting even without the day-7 trigger.

---

#### 5. Wire campsite art — campsite layouts are invisible (Est: 2 hours)

`TryPlaceCampsite()` looks up `campsite_fire`, `campsite_lantern`,
`campsite_table`, `campsite_chest` via `FindArtNode()` which returns null for all
four — campsite layouts silently disappear. `campfire_new.png` is already on disk
but not registered to any ID.

**Fix:**
1. In the relevant `ResourceNodeDefinition` or decoration JSON, register
   `campsite_fire` → resolves to `campfire_new.png`.
2. Generate or promote `campsite_lantern`, `campsite_table`, `campsite_chest` PNGs
   to `Resources/Decorations/` (to PixelLab style).
This makes every settlement campsite visually present.

---

#### 6. Mountain tile family — complete the stratification (Est: art drop only)

`mountain.json` references `mountain_stone_08` through `mountain_stone_14` (7
base IDs + `_new` remix variants). None exist on disk. The C# fallback
(`MountainSurfaceVariant()`) fires instead, making all mountain elevations use
the same 4-tile pool (`stone_scree`, `stone_mossy`, `stone_cracked`,
`stone_snowcap`). All high-elevation terrain looks identical.

**Fix:** Drop 7 PixelLab mountain stone tile PNGs named `mountain_stone_08.png`
through `mountain_stone_14.png` into `Resources/Tiles/`. No code change needed —
`TileSpriteResolver` picks them up automatically. Unlocks the full JSON
stratification (different stone looks per elevation band).

---

#### 7. Autosave trigger (Est: 1 hour)

`FoundationBootstrap.Save()` is a complete, robust implementation but is only
called explicitly via `PauseMenu` (or on quit). There is no periodic autosave.

**Fix:** In `FoundationBootstrap.Update()`, track a `_autosaveTimer` (default
5 min) and call `Save(DefaultSavePath)` when it fires. Add a serialized
`autosaveIntervalMinutes` field so it's tunable per session config.

### TIER 2 — Medium effort, high production feel

#### 8. Add portal-traverse and dungeon-enter camera moments (Est: 1 day)

Currently there is no camera reaction to dungeon entry. `FoundationDungeonPortal
System` handles the transition silently.

**Suggested:** On portal activation — short 0.3 s camera zoom-in to the portal
`(cameraSize - 2f)`, 0.4 s fade-to-black, scene switch, fade-in. On
`IsoCoreFoundation` load with dungeon save data, camera briefly zooms out from
tight to default (0.5 s ease). These are camera animation coroutines with no
new dependencies.

---

#### 9. Player attack animation state (Est: 1 day)

`PlayerAnimator` has only 4 rows (down/up/left/right) for idle+walk. There is no
attack, hurt, or die row. The player character never visually reacts to combat.

**Suggested approach:** Add `PlayerAnimationState` enum (Idle/Walk/Run/Attack/Hurt)
to `PlayerAnimator`. Wire `PlayerInteraction` tool swing → `SetState(Attack)`.
Wire `FoundationPlayerStats.TakeDamage` → `SetState(Hurt, 0.3s)`. Revert to
Idle/Walk after duration. Requires matching sprite sheet rows, but can fall back
gracefully to Walk row until art exists.

---

#### 10. Existing mobs not retroactively boosted at nightfall (Est: 0.5 days)

`MobSpawner.SpawnMob()` calls `ApplyNightDanger()` at spawn time. Mobs alive when
night falls get no boost — only newly-spawned mobs are dangerous. This makes the
day-to-night transition feel flat for the player's current surroundings.

**Fix:** In `DayNightSystem.OnDayCompleted` (or a new `OnNightBegan` event), call
`ApplyNightDanger()` on all live `Mob` instances where `_nightDanger == false`.
Reverse on dawn via an `OnDayBegan` event.

---

#### 11. System message visual hierarchy (Est: 0.5 days)

Mid-session System messages arrive as the same toast style as a mundane inventory
pickup. The `ClassAssign` and `LevelUp` message types have their own `MessageType`
enum values but `BuildToast()` doesn't visually differentiate them beyond the
accent-bar colour.

**Fix:** For `MessageType.ClassAssign` and `LevelUp`, prefix the toast text with
`"◆ SYSTEM"` in a bold `ContentSizeFitter` label and increase the font size from
14 to 16. Zero new components needed.

---

#### 12. Campfire VFX (Est: 1 hour code + art drop)

`FoundationCampfireAnimator.cs` exists and animates the campfire sprite, but there
is no particle emitter on campfire props. A campfire with no visible fire or ember
particles undercuts the "safety radius" feel.

**Fix:** In `FoundationCampfireAnimator.Awake()`, spawn a procedural
`ParticleSystem` as a child GO with a warm ember config (upward burst, orange/red
gradient, small size, short lifetime, loop). `WorldFx` already shows the
pattern for procedural particles — this is a ~30-line addition.

---

#### 13. Loading screen progress indicator (Est: 1 hour)

The loading screen shows a title and a tip but no animated progress indicator.
Long world-gen loads could appear frozen.

**Fix:** Add a pulsing diamond `Image` (or ellipsis dots using `DOTween`/coroutine)
to `LoadingScreen.Build()`. Alternatively wire `IsoWorld`'s chunk-generation loop
to report `0..1` progress via a static event that `LoadingScreen` subscribes to.

---

#### 14. Fuel display in crafting UI (Est: 1 hour)

`CraftingRecipeDetails.fuelInfo` is populated by the crafting adapter but
`CraftingView.RefreshDetails()` does not render it — there is no fuel label in the
detail pane build. The cancel button logs `"wire to adapter"` and does nothing.

**Fix:** Add `_fuelLabel` Text to `Build()` in `CraftingView`, populate from
`d.fuelInfo`. Add `ICraftingViewModel.CancelJob()` and wire the cancel button.

---

### TIER 3 — Polish / ongoing

#### 15. Biome ambient audio (currently silent)

`WorldAudioController.SetBiomeSource()` is wired and will crossfade ambient by
biome if `Resources/Audio/Ambient/Biome/<id>.wav` files exist. None do. Dropping
5 ambient loops (meadow wind, forest birds, snow wind, beach waves, dungeon drip)
would transform the world's atmosphere with zero code changes.

#### 16. Mob death frames deferred destruction

`Mob.TakeMobDamage()` destroys the mob immediately. `_die` animation arrays are
loaded but never play. Defer `Destroy()` by 0.35 s (one `StartCoroutine`) and
switch the renderer to `_die[0]` frame first. Immediate visible improvement.

#### 17. Look-ahead on camera

`FoundationBootstrap` uses `SmoothDamp` at 0.15 s but there is no look-ahead — the
camera trails behind. Adding a 0.5-world-unit offset in the player's `MoveDir`
reduces the "chasing" feel. Single-line change to the follow target calculation.

#### 18. HUD stamina bar

`FoundationHudAdapter.Hunger01` returns hard-coded `0f`. Wire to
`_stats?.Stamina01 ?? 0f` (one line). Either makes the bar meaningful or hide it.

#### 19. Void ceremony reachability (interim)

Before the full §1 fix, subscribe `TrialWeekManager_Phase2.OnPlayerTrialEnd` to
`ClassAssignmentView.Show(new PlaceholderClassAssignmentViewModel())` in
`GameHudInitializer`. This makes the ceremony reachable on day 7 even without the
real adapter — immediate playtestability.

## What this project does NOT need right now

- New vision features (guild, boats, horses, towns). The systems exist; close the
  existing loops first.
- A new save system. The current one is complete and robust.
- A new worldgen rewrite. It is sophisticated and working.
- More UI panels. The 9-tab panel covers all content; polish what exists.
- A new art direction. PixelLab is decided and mostly wired; fill the specific gaps
  listed above.
