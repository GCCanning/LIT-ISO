Wall Slicer

Each source is a back-corner where a left wall and a right wall meet. Drag the seam to the exact column where they join (default = centre); the left/right previews update live. Download each half — they save as clean separate wall pieces (tight-cropped, transparent). Then drop the PNGs into assets/tavern_pack/ and tell Claude "walls sliced" to register them.
→ Interior Editor
Tip: the seam is shared across all three (the art is aligned). If one needs a different cut, slice it, nudge the seam, and re-download just that one.
